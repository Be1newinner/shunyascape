# Battleground asset credits

- Props, guns (Kar98k-style bolt-action rifle, service pistol), loot boxes, rocks, logs, ground / wall textures:
  [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), CC0. Re-encoded (meshopt, WebP, simplified) for the web.
- Trees and bushes: generated with [EZ-Tree](https://github.com/dgreenheck/ez-tree) (MIT) and baked to GLB; bark/leaf textures from the same project.
- Soldier character and Idle/Walk/Run animations: `Soldier.glb` from the [three.js examples](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf) (originally a Mixamo character).
- M416-style carbine, grass tufts, sky, effects and sounds: made in code.
